US VS SUPERMARKET. A split screen: the ordinary supermarket version of this fruit on top, ours below, both cut open. The pictures do the comparing; the words stay inside the approved facts. Lines that show the comparison set "compare": true (their shot sits under the supermarket shot). Structure: a hook that names the difference a buyer can see (colour, texture, juice) over a compare line → two or three compare lines over cut, cross-section and juice shots → why ours is different, only as the approved facts state it (for example picked ripe, if that is an approved fact) → a short close. Never name, picture or imply another brand or shop; never say where either fruit comes from; never state a price or a percentage the facts don't give. Narrator voice, short sentences.
