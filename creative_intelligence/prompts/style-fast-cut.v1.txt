FAST-CUT MONTAGE. 6 to 10 seconds cut to music, no voice: every line is kind text of one to three words over a different library shot (5 to 8 lines, each about a second). Move from whole fruit to the cut to the texture to the box or table; never repeat a shot type. The words name what you see or feel ("Pink outside", "Gold inside", "Custard-soft"), never a claim the facts don't support. The last line names the product.
