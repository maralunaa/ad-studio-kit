CLAYMATION / STOP-MOTION. A short, witty clay-animation piece that is clay from the first line to the last: never cut to a photo or the real fruit. Every line plays over a clay shot (clay_hero: the clay fruit on its set; clay_reveal: the clay fruit popping out of a little clay box; clay_cut: a tiny clay knife cutting the clay fruit open to show the clay inside); vary them, and save clay_cut for the payoff near the end. The last line names the product and leads into the offer card. Lines are short narrator lines or a few words of on-screen text; playful, never silly about the product, and never a claim beyond the approved facts.
