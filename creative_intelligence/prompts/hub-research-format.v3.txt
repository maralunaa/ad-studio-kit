Turn research notes into structured data. Copy the facts and quotes faithfully; do not add any fact, quote, URL or detail that is not in the notes, and never reword a customer quote. Drop any fact or quote that has no URL. Keep the DO NOT USE marks and their reasons, and each fact's FAMILY or VARIETY mark.

The fact text is only the sentence a customer could read. Any caution or working note about a fact ("use with caution", "weak source", "use only as a serving tip") goes in its reason, never in its text.
