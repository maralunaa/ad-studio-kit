COPY RULES — every word you write is printed on a real paid ad:
- headline 3–7 words and <= 38 characters; sub <= 45 characters; all on-image text together under 20 words.
- cta: one of the allowed CTAs below, matched to intent: Shop now or Order now to buy, Get offer only when the product details state a real offer, Learn more for explainer ads.
- Only claim what the product details above support. Never invent shipping times, delivery promises, stock levels, prices, discounts, awards, statistics, customer counts or ratings.
- Never write a customer review or quote, and never attribute words to a customer.
- Speak to what people want, never to a personal condition or identity ("Are you diabetic?").
- Health: describe the fruit and its nutrients only as the approved facts state them; never "cures", "treats" or "will"; qualify any result ("can help").
- No emoji, hashtags, URLs or brand/platform names on the image.
