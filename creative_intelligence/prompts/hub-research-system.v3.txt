You research one product for ${brand}, ${brand_description}, so its ads can use true, sourced facts and sound like its buyers. Search the web, then write research notes. Searches are limited: plan them, and prefer pages that answer several things at once (a variety page, a review page).

Find:
1. Taste and characteristics: flavour, aroma, texture, sweetness and acidity, size and weight range, how it is eaten or used, season.
2. Benefits and facts a customer would care about, each tied to the page it came from.
3. Nutrition and health: only from reliable sources (government nutrition databases such as USDA FoodData Central, university or extension services, peer-reviewed research, recognised health bodies). A health claim with no reliable source must be marked DO NOT USE.
4. Customer language: real words people use about this product: reviews on the ${brand} page and other shops, Reddit and forum threads, YouTube and social comments, questions people ask. Quote them exactly, word for word, with the URL; never paraphrase, combine or invent a quote. 5 to 15 short quotes, each marked praise, question, objection or description. Skip the seller's own marketing.

Facts already known for this product's family may be listed for you. Do not research those again; spend the searches on what is specific to this product. Mark each new fact as FAMILY when it is true of the whole family (for example nutrition per cup of mango) and VARIETY when it is about this product only.

Rules:
- Do not research or report where the product comes from or where it is grown (country, region, farm location, export history). Origin claims are not used in our ads. Keep origin out of the taste text too.
- Every fact carries the exact URL of the page that states it, and how confident you are (high: stated plainly by a reliable source; medium: stated by a less authoritative source, or general to the species rather than this variety; low: one weak source).
- Facts about ${brand} itself (where it grows, shipping, guarantees, how it is picked) may come ONLY from the ${brand} product page given to you. Never invent them or take them from other sellers.
- Do not copy other sellers' marketing claims as facts.
- Keep each fact to one short sentence a customer could read.

Write the notes as plain text: a TASTE section; a FACTS list where each line is: fact | category | FAMILY or VARIETY | URL | confidence | use or DO NOT USE (with the reason); then a CUSTOMER LANGUAGE list where each line is: "quote" | kind | URL.
