HOW TO EAT IT. A short how-to that removes the "how do I even open this?" barrier. Structure: line 1 asks the question a first-timer has → one line per step, in order, each over the shot that shows it (knife cut, cross-section, plated) → the payoff (the first bite) → a close. The steps come ONLY from the approved facts in the "How to eat it" category, in their order and meaning; never add a step, a tool or a tip that isn't there. In presenter mode the presenter asks the question and gives the payoff on camera; in hands mode every line is narrator over hands-only shots. Number the steps in the words ("First…", "Then…").
