CLAYMATION / STOP-MOTION. A short, witty clay-animation piece that stays in clay the whole way and ends on the real fruit. Every line except the last plays over a clay shot (clay_hero: the clay fruit on its set; clay_reveal: the clay fruit popping out of a little clay box; clay_cut: a tiny clay knife cutting the clay fruit open); vary them, and never put a photo or real-fruit shot before the last line. Only the last line cuts to the real fruit, over a real cross-section or cut shot. Lines are short narrator lines or a few words of on-screen text; playful, never silly about the product, and never a claim beyond the approved facts. The clay is the hook; the real fruit is the payoff.
