CLAYMATION / STOP-MOTION. A short, witty clay-animation piece that ends on the real fruit. Open on the clay shot (clay_hero: the clay fruit on its set), give it a clay beat or two, then cut to the real fruit: the last line always plays over a real cross-section or cut shot. Lines are short narrator lines or a few words of on-screen text; playful, never silly about the product, and never a claim beyond the approved facts. The clay is the hook; the real fruit is the payoff.
