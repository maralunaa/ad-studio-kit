FRUIT STORY (the flagship). Structure: a presenter hook on camera (line 1, kind presenter) → an orchard or tree shot with the narrator → on the tree → three different cut, macro or texture shots, one per line → the presenter on camera on why it's different (kind presenter) → a short closing line over the best shot. The offer card is drawn after the last line. Presenter lines are short (one or two sentences, under 8 seconds each); narrator lines carry the sensory detail.
